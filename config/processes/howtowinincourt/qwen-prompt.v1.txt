How to Control Judges

Insight Based on Experience

First Rule

The first rule when dealing with judges is that they are human ... though at times you may think the judge in your case is a gorilla wearing a black robe!

Some are.

Most are not.

Everyday you find yourself in front of one in the courtroom, stop a moment to recognize the judge woke up this morning just as you did, has family and loved ones just as you do, and is faced with the same mortality that faces us all. The judge or judge's family member may have a life-threatening disease weighing on his or her mind. The judge may have another case later in the day that will require dire decisions far more important than the ones involved in your case (though decisions in your case seem more important to you).

And, sometimes, the judge may care more about you and seeing that justice is served in your case than you have yet imagined possible. The judge may have decided to rule in your favor even before hearing the facts or researching the law, depending on what the judge thinks of you as a person of honesty and integrity or what the judge knows about your opponent's reputation for dishonesty and deceit. It happens!
