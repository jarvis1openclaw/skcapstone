# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SKLegal respectful, safe, and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, disability, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Protect client confidentiality, identity material, and private security reports.
- State honestly what a test, review, model, or cryptographic control proves.
- Accept correction and preserve provenance so mistakes can be repaired.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, publishing private information, public
disclosure of an unfixed vulnerability, and knowingly misrepresenting authority or
evidence are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact on the
[`smilinTux`](https://github.com/smilinTux) organization profile. Maintainers may warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participation based on impact and pattern,
while protecting reporter confidentiality.

This policy applies in repository spaces and whenever someone officially represents
the project. It is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
