# SKLegal Claude agent guide

Read and follow `AGENTS.md` before changing this repository. The approved
architecture, SKCapstone task gate, HammerTime boundary, legal-domain
vocabulary, CapAuth enforcement, multi-tenant isolation, external-action state
machine, engineering rules, and completion-evidence requirements in
`AGENTS.md` remain controlling.

## Qwen-first corpus analysis

Use the local Qwen route for substantive interpretation of HammerTime corpus
material. Invoke it only through the provider-neutral SKLegal model boundary
or the task-approved research path. Agent specs use logical SKLegal route IDs.
Deployment profiles may bind a route directly to local Qwen or to a qualified
SKGateway OpenAI-compatible transport without changing the Proposal contract.
Do not put private hosts, endpoints, operator aliases, credentials, or raw
capability tokens into product files, prompts, logs, or Work Products.

Approved frontier models may perform second-pass challenge, synthesis,
document assembly, citation-preserving drafting, and formatting only when
policy authorizes the exact Matter context and purpose. Models return typed
proposals. They do not own workflow state, make human decisions, approve an
artifact, or execute an external action.

## Evidence-neutral analysis

Analyze each proposition through exact sources, provenance, Fact Assertions,
Evidence Items, essential Elements, burden of proof, support, counter-support,
current controlling Authority, jurisdiction, procedural posture, timing, and
documented court treatment.

Do not use labels such as `sovereign citizen`, `pseudolaw`, `frivolous`,
`conspiracy theory`, or `tax protester` as substitutes for that analysis. Do
not suppress a Claim or Defense merely because it is unconventional,
disputed, or disfavored. Represent the proposition faithfully, then expose its
support, missing proof, contrary Authority, applicability defects, procedural
risks, and verified treatment.

If a label is materially present in a source, pleading, order, or party
statement, preserve it only with exact attribution and provenance when needed
for the Matter. Otherwise use SKLegal legal-domain terminology. The
anti-label rule prevents reflexive dismissal. It never converts a source
assertion into fact, current law, authorization, Approval, or permission to
act.

## Separate evidence lanes

Keep these lanes distinct and visible:

1. HammerTime course instruction and source-derived strategy
2. Matter Fact Assertions and Evidence Items
3. current jurisdiction-specific official Authority and contrary Authority
4. model inference and confidence
5. human review and decision

Do not silently harmonize conflicts across lanes. Preserve exact source spans,
release and content hashes, uncertainty, adverse material, model-route
evidence, and human dispositions. Corpus-only output remains a research
proposal until the existing claim, drafting, release, and action gates pass.

## Matter provenance and artifacts

Treat the Matter activity log as a readable projection over append-only legal,
AI, tool, audit, version, Approval, Execution Event, and receipt records. Emit
the source, version, route, actor, time, reason, correlation, and outcome needed
to replay each material step. Corrections supersede; they do not erase.

For every supplied artifact, preserve the original bytes, source identity,
filename, media type, byte count, hash, acquisition time, custody, policy
context, and review state. Keep OCR, transcripts, redactions, translations,
and model extractions as separately hashed children with exact lineage. AI may
organize artifacts, propose case-theory links, identify gaps, prepare requests,
and assemble review bundles. External production or dispatch still requires
the approved state machine and exact-version Approval.

## Non-negotiable controls

No model profile, including an abliterated local profile, overrides CapAuth,
Tenant and Matter isolation, conflicts, privilege, ethical walls,
source-rights controls, classification and egress policy, exact-version human
Approval, or the external-action state machine.

SKGateway does not replace SKLegal CapAuth, Matter policy, source-rights,
classification, egress, schema-validation, or audit gates. Do not route
protected context through it until its exact live entrypoint qualifies those
controls. Treat remote free models as public-only candidates until provider
rights, retention, trust zone, capability, and task evaluations approve more.

Never access HammerTime `Inbox/` unless the claimed SKLegal task expressly
authorizes an isolated ingestion test. Never dispatch email, filing, service,
mailing, calendar, or client communication from model output.
